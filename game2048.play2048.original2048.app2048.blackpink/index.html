"Blackpink 2048 Game" (the "App") is a puzzle game published by Snuri. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and the choices you have. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information the App collects

  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token so the App can send game notifications.
  • Advertising data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to serve, measure and personalize ads, and to prevent fraud.
  • App settings. Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations use an installation ID to deliver app settings.
  • Data kept only on your device. Coins, unlocked skins, settings and your saved game are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

How the information is used

  • To run the game, save your statistics and show the leaderboard.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To keep the App working, fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information.

Third-party services

The App uses services provided by Google, which process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) — Firebase privacy information

Your choices

  • Ad consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can review or change your choice in the App under Settings > Privacy and Ads.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings.
  • Notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Data deletion. Uninstalling the App removes the data stored on your device. To delete your game records stored in Firestore, email us from the address below; we will delete them within 30 days.

Game records are kept while you use the App and deleted on request. Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure.
